Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 October 2026

Stories is a private tool used only by its owner to publish videos to the owner's own TikTok and YouTube accounts. It is not offered to the public.

What we access

When the owner signs in with TikTok or Google, the app receives an access token and basic account information (such as display name and account ID) and the permissions needed to upload videos and read the account's video statistics.

How we use it

This information is used only to upload the owner's videos and to read performance statistics for those videos. It is not sold, shared with third parties or used for advertising.

Storage and security

Tokens are stored in encrypted secret storage and are never published. Video performance data is stored privately to improve posting schedules.

Retention and deletion

Access can be revoked at any time from the TikTok or Google account settings. Stored tokens and data are deleted when the owner stops using the app.

Contact

Questions: open an issue at github.com/ST1CKYB1ACK/stories.
